Sonny Hall is a painter, actor, model and poet from South London. Hall made his debut on the poetry scene in 2019 by self-publishing the collection of poems The Blues Comes With Good News, which was then picked up by prestigious British publishing house Hodder & Stoughton, before founding his own imprint, Blue Beggar Books.

A modern polymath, Hall began his modelling career in 2015 and has continued to write and paint alongside it ever since. His work as an ambassador for Saint Laurent led to Anthony Vaccarello inviting him to publish his second book with Saint Laurent Editions, titled On the Soft Edge, which included a selection of poems, paintings and drawings.

He spoke at Trinity College and St John’s College on tradition in poetry, confession and form, alongside the literary society of Cambridge University. In summer 2026, he released his third collection of poems, Double Double, with Heartworm Press.

Hall now occupies a distinctive position within contemporary British creative culture. His practice is defined by a commitment to experimentation, emotional candour and collaboration: a continual search for new ways to translate lived experience into poetry, painting, image and film. His forthcoming work continues this multidisciplinary trajectory, including new poetry and a major collaborative project with Nick Knight combining text, film and visual art, set as a book due to be published by Wasmuth Verlag and an exhibition in early 2027.
