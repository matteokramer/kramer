Yury Belyavskiy (b. 2003) is a visual artist working in sculpture using mainly silicone. His practice is rooted in transformation, anatomy, and the materiality of the human form. His work explores gender expression, beauty standards, and femininity in society, examining how these constructs shape identity and the body through both surgical and aesthetic intervention. Yury’s practice spans installation, sculpture, and documentation, engaging with themes of vanity and transformation across multiple media. Currently based in Berlin, Yury studies Fine Arts at UdK in Monica Bonvicini’s class.
