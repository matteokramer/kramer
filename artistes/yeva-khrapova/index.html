Yeva Khrapova

Entrée 015 · Magenta Made Me Hardcore

Née2003, Ukraine
RésidenceParis
Au registreMagenta Made Me Hardcore · KR03

My name is Yeva Khrapova, I am 23 years old, and I am a student in École de Beaux Arts de Paris in Tim Eitel’s studio.

I come from Dnipro, an industrial city in eastern Ukraine, which I left at the beginning of the war to move to Paris and apply for an art studies.

In my work I’m interested in a certain feeling of melancholy, a nostalgia connected not only to lost places, but also to a certain feelings, that started to reveal themselves only with time.

I work from personal archives and memories shot on Polaroid camera. These images are the way for me to remain connected with the past while transforming it in the present reality. I made photos of simple still life scenes with everyday objects: flowers, bedsheets, lingerie—that become a kind of intimate setting for my paintings.

Light is an essential element in my work, especially flash from camera. In photography, flash reveals details, but in my paintings, it almost does the opposite: light erases forms, blurs faces, and creates a kind of mysterious veil, and leaves a room for unexpected.

In my paintings, I work with a combination of oil paint and charcoal. Charcoal allows me to create deep, soft, and diffuse shadows that feel almost unstable. With oil paint, I use thin and transparent layers, almost like petals of the flower. This allows me to achieve a luminous surface without heaviness.
