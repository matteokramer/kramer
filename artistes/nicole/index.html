Nicole

Entrée 011 · Magenta Made Me Hardcore

Née1997, Quito
RésidenceParis
Au registreMagenta Made Me Hardcore · KR03

Nicole (b. 1997, Quito, Ecuador) is a Paris-based interdisciplinary and conceptual artist whose work examines how the female body is constructed, displayed, and instrumentalized within systems of desire, value, and representation. Working across performance, video, and installation, her practice engages with the aesthetics of advertising, eroticism, and the art market, often using stillness, staging, and theatrical poise to reveal the mechanics of the gaze.

Recent performances include Represented (Art Basel Paris, Grand Palais, 2025). Her ongoing performance series At Your Feet (2025–) has unfolded through multiple iterations, including Stand By (Marcelle Alix, Paris, 2025), Count the Days Until (Bétonsalon, Paris, 2025), Linger (Paris Internationale, Paris, 2025), Be Ready (Shmorévaz, Paris, 2025), and Be Patient (Yes AI Can Festival, Rostock, 2026). Other performances include Accueil, Table d’entrée (Galerie municipale Jean-Collet, Ivry-sur-Seine, 2026), Bonita Bonice (WIG, Mexico City, 2026), El Trencito (Rudimento, Quito, 2026), TV Buddha (Mira Art Fair, Paris, 2024), and Amor y Besitos (DOC, Paris, 2024).

Nicole holds a Master’s degree in Fine Arts from the Beaux-Arts de Paris. Recent exhibitions include Puerto Adentro (Museo Centro Cultural de Manta, Manta, 2026), El fantasma de Tennessee (Marcelle Alix, Paris, 2025), Coller l’oreille aux colimaçons (FRAC Île-de-France, Romainville, 2024), J’aime la vie quand t’es là (Exo Exo, Paris, 2023), and Semblable à un petit os de seiche (Bétonsalon, Paris, 2023).

She appears on the cover of Art Interrupted, Línea Recta edition (2025), and her work was published in Pour toi (2023).
